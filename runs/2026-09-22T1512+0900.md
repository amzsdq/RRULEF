## Finding
The prior near-future anchor did produce a subsequent run, but with at least 216.813 seconds of lateness relative to the requested anchor in the available scheduler evidence. This does not invalidate near-future re-anchoring: jitter is an exogenous cost unless it can be reduced without sacrificing active work. Optimizing scheduled-time accuracy itself would be a proxy objective and can conflict with the invariant.

The correct primary quantity is:

`idle_gap_sec = next_actual_useful_work_start - previous_actual_useful_work_end`

Wake lateness is retained only as a diagnostic component of idle gap.
