## Finding
The scheduler can invoke this recurring automation materially earlier than the written DTSTART: the live last_run_time was 13:06:46.427 KST while the prior intended DTSTART was 13:12:00 KST, a -313.573 s offset. Therefore DTSTART cannot currently be treated as an exact lower bound on invocation time. This is useful for utilization because an early wake reduces idle gap, but it invalidates any controller that assumes requested wake == actual wake.

The immediate optimization implication is to measure observed invocation timestamps rather than infer idle time from schedule targets. Do not add a larger safety delay in response to this sample; that would increase idle time without evidence of benefit.
